# Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

HEIC to JPG converts photos locally on your Mac. The application does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or network requests.

## Photos and metadata

The app reads only files you choose or open with it and writes JPG copies beside the originals. Your original photos are kept. Files are processed on your Mac and are not uploaded.

**Keep photo metadata** is enabled by default. When enabled, supported capture-date, camera, and location metadata is copied into the JPG. When disabled, those fields are omitted; image orientation and color information remain so the photo displays correctly. Metadata included in a JPG will travel with that file if you share it yourself.

## Local settings

JPEG quality and metadata preferences are stored locally. The sandboxed version also stores security-scoped bookmarks for folders you authorize, allowing future JPGs to be saved there without repeating the folder-selection step. These bookmarks stay on your Mac. Choose **HEIC to JPG → Forget Saved Folder Access** to remove the saved bookmarks. The action is available when conversions are idle.

The recent-conversion list is held in memory and is cleared when you quit or click Clear. Clearing the list does not delete photos or JPGs.

## Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party services. Downloads, purchases, diagnostics you choose to share with Apple, and App Store infrastructure are governed separately by Apple's policies.

## Questions and changes

Use the support channel listed with the released app to ask privacy questions. Before the first public release, the maintainer must publish this policy at a public URL and provide a support contact. This repository currently contains the policy text for that purpose.

If the app's data practices change, update this policy and the App Store privacy answers before publishing the change.
